Connect to Your Real Home Wi-Fi

Right now you are viewing sample demo devices. To scan and block real devices on your actual home Wi-Fi:

Step 1 — Run on a PC or Raspberry Pi at home
sudo python3 server.py --port 8080 --dns-port 53
Step 2 — Set Router DNS to that computer's IP

In your router settings (usually 192.168.1.1 → DHCP/DNS), set Primary DNS to the computer running J.A.R.V.I.S. Any device you click Block & Disconnect on will have 100% of its internet lookups cut off.
